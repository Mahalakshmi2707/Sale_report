This interactive Sales & Performance Executive Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of global revenue streams, product profitability, and regional growth across $1.2\text{bn}$ in total revenue and $64\text{K}$ orders.
Developed with a modular top-level navigation framework—spanning Executive Overview & Trends, Product & Channel Performance, and Geographic & Customer Insights—the system converts complex transactional data into actionable strategic metrics.
Key insights reveal that Wholesale is the dominant distribution channel, generating $54.06\%$ of revenue ($\$668.2\text{M}$) at a market-leading $38.01\%$ profit margin.
Geographic performance is spearheaded by the West region ($\$372.1\text{M}$ revenue) with California individually driving $\$228.79\text{M}$, while Product 26 and Product 9 lead in total revenue generation and margin efficiency, respectively.

The technical stack driving this dynamic visualization suite leverages robust data modeling, custom analytical measures, and UX/UI optimization.
Data cleaning, transformation, and DAX modeling handle key measures such as Revenue per Order ($\$19.3\text{K}$), custom spending tier binning (`OrderValue bins`), and cross-dimensional customer rank toggles.
Visual assets—including scatter plots for revenue vs. margin positioning, area density curves for profit momentum, and donut scorecards for channel efficiency—were built using a unified high-contrast executive theme.
Dynamic slicers enable real-time top and bottom $5$ filtering across customers and states, offering business stakeholders immediate visibility into key growth drivers and operational bottlenecks.
